寶 - Treasure

What is the weight of a memory? This film captures the core of our "Treasure Hunt" project by presenting the unguarded voices of Zhoushuzhun's villagers. Emerging from our photography project, which explored the deep connection between personal objects and memory, this film moves beyond the artefacts to focus on the people themselves. In this compilation, four residents speak directly of their lived experiences—their historical plight and their present realities. The accompanying visuals of their daily routines ground their words in a powerful, tangible context. This film serves as a digital archive of personal history, a testament to resilience, and an act of preserving intangible heritage against the tide of time.

Acknowledgement

The video was filmed in August 2025 during the "Interdisciplinary Capacity Building Workshops in an Ageing Village". Workshop funded by HKU Gallant Ho Experiential Learning Fund. Workshop structure guided by HKU The Horizons Office.
