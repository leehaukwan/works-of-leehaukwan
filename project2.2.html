Midnight in Hong Kong

A Solitary Wanderer’s Search for Self in Hong Kong’s Day and Night - Inspired by Midnight in Paris, this cinematic video explores the theme of urban alienation and the golden age fallacy. It follows a solitary wanderer—me—lost in Hong Kong’s mesmerising cityscape, grappling with identity and yearning for a romanticised past. The subject centres on my introspective journey through the city’s dual nature: a chaotic metropolis by day and a reflective dreamscape at night. The video channels my emotions through the “golden age thinking” fallacy— Quoting Midnight in Paris, “Nostalgia is denial – denial of the painful present… and the name for this fallacy is called golden age thinking – the erroneous notion that a different time period is better than the one one’s living in – it’s a flaw in the romantic imagination of those people who find it difficult to cope with the present.
