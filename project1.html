尋寶記 - Treasure Hunt

我們賦予個人物品什麼樣的意義？有些人可能僅以物質層面來看待物品，但我們的團隊深入探討了中國河南鄭州周山村中，個人物品所承載的深層象徵意義。在我們的研究中，我們將物品視為記憶的容器——跨越時間與世代，承載着個人與集體的歷史。

在記憶可能被扭曲或重塑的社會時代背景下，我們試圖理解村民如何通過物質文化來感知和保存他們的過去。通過走進他們的家園，進行深入的對話，我們探索了他們賦予自己物品的意義。隨着鄉村人口外流和城市化進程加速，這些承載意義的物品將何去何從？它們會被保存、傳承，還是被遺棄？

沒有人知道答案，但通過記錄這些物品所體現的未經修飾、情感豐富且常被忽視的記憶，我們旨在捕捉一個正在消逝的文化遺產。我們工作的另一個有趣面向在於，同一村莊的人們對他們製作、使用或收到的物品，賦予了不同程度的重要性。

雖然這些物品的物理命運仍不確定，但將環繞着擁有者的這些有意義的物件數碼化，讓我們得以為未來守護他們的故事。我們的項目命名為「尋寶記」，旨在突顯他們尋獲珍寶，以及將所有這些寶藏匯聚在同一張影像中的過程。

What importance do we assign to personal objects? While some might view objects from a purely materialistic perspective, our research group investigated the deeper meanings and symbolic layers associated with personal items in the rural village of Zhoushan, Henan, China. In our study, we approached objects not merely as utilitarian or aesthetic artefacts, but as vessels of memory—guardians of personal and collective histories across time and generations.

In a socio-temporal context where memories can be distorted or reshaped, we sought to understand how villagers perceive and preserve their past through material culture. By entering their homes and engaging in intimate conversations, we explored the significance they attach to their possessions. As rural exodus and urbanisation accelerate, what becomes of these meaningful objects? Will they be preserved, passed down, or discarded?

No one knows, but by documenting the raw, emotional, and often overlooked dimensions of memory embodied in these items, we aim to capture a vanishing cultural heritage. Another interesting dimension of our work lies in the differences in how people from the same village assign different degrees of importance to objects they have made, used or received.

While the physical fate of these objects remains uncertain, digitalising these meaningful artefacts surrounding their owners allows us to safeguard their stories for the future. Our project is named Treasure Hunt to highlight the process of searching for their meaningful object and the gathering of all these treasures on one single picture.
